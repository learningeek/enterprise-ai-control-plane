# Goals

- Give enterprises real-time visibility into AI cost and usage, attributable to department/app/user.
- Provide policy-based governance over AI usage, including MCP/agent tool access.
- Detect shadow AI (unapproved tool usage) and risky behavior (e.g. confidential data to public models).
- Forecast AI spend and recommend cost optimizations (e.g. model right-sizing).
- Support on-premise, customer-cloud, and SaaS deployment from one codebase.
