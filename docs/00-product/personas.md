# Personas

## Executive sponsor (CFO / Head of AI)
Wants: monthly AI spend, trend, and forecast by department. Cares about ROI and budget predictability.

## Engineering leader
Wants: model usage, latency, and cost per application/team. Cares about not blocking developer velocity.

## Security / governance lead
Wants: policy violations, shadow AI, MCP/data access risk. Cares about auditability and blocking behavior.

## Platform/AI team (internal buyer who runs this tool day to day)
Wants: a single control plane instead of stitching together provider dashboards + spreadsheets.
