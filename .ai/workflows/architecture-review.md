# Workflow: architecture review

**Agent:** architect. **Read first:** context.md, docs/02-architecture/*, relevant ADRs.
**Steps:** identify the architectural question, check for an existing ADR, propose options with
tradeoffs, record the decision as a new ADR if one is made. **Done when:** an ADR exists or the question
is explicitly logged as a `<!-- DECISION NEEDED -->` in the relevant doc.
